## blush_sage

**display_name:** Blush Sage

**digest:** Tier A / 推荐优先 — general refined diagrams, AI workflows, method comparisons, polished explanatory figures.

**mood:** Soft editorial warmth with a sage and blue counterweight. Refined, contemporary, and calm without becoming sugary.

**use_when:** General-purpose diagrams that need to feel designed rather than default: AI workflows, product systems, research summaries, process maps, method comparisons, and mixed qualitative/result explanations.

**avoid_when:** Strict accessibility-critical categorical comparisons with many equal categories, hard compliance documents that require a conservative institutional tone, or kid-facing material that should be openly playful.

**reference_anchors:** Modern editorial data graphics, Linear-style documentation, refined product explainers, contemporary research presentation decks.

**balance_guidance:** Rose and sand provide the warm base (~35-45%). Sage and cyan keep the palette fresh and credible (~25-35%). Indigo is the precise focal accent for one or two primary nodes. Keep berry/rose strokes meaningful rather than decorative.

**common_mistakes:** Turning every node pink, which makes the diagram sentimental. Using indigo everywhere instead of as a focal anchor. Pairing this palette with heavy black outlines; the dark strokes already carry enough structure.
