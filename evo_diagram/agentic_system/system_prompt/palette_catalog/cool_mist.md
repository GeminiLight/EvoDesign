## cool_mist

**display_name:** Cool Mist

**digest:** Tier A / 推荐优先 — pure cool-tone maps, ordered conditions, density-style structures, calm analytical diagrams.

**mood:** Pale cyan, mist blue, and lavender in a controlled cool register. Airy, precise, and quiet.

**use_when:** Cool-tone diagrams, embedding maps, ordered conditions, response surfaces, analysis workflows, technical overviews, and diagrams where warmth would distract from structure.

**avoid_when:** Customer journeys, emotionally warm topics, explicit success/failure narratives, or diagrams that need strong categorical contrast among many equal actors.

**reference_anchors:** Scientific visualization with restrained cool tones, frosted product UI, contemporary data notebooks, research-system maps.

**balance_guidance:** Cyan and mist carry the base (~45%). Blue and lavender provide progression and differentiation. Violet is the only focal accent. Keep all fills light; make hierarchy through stroke and placement rather than saturation.

**common_mistakes:** Adding warm colors from another palette. Using violet on too many nodes. Making the canvas blue; the palette needs a near-white field to stay crisp.
