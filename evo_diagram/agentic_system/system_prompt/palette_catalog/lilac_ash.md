## lilac_ash

**display_name:** Lilac Ash

**digest:** Tier A / 推荐优先 — human evaluation, qualitative findings, preference maps, survey and mixed-result diagrams.

**mood:** Dusty lilac and ash gray with a muted coral pulse. Soft, intelligent, and reflective.

**use_when:** Human evaluation diagrams, qualitative findings, survey flows, preference comparisons, critique loops, UX research synthesis, and mixed-result explanations where a softer intellectual tone helps.

**avoid_when:** Dense engineering architecture, finance/legal materials, or diagrams that need a very high-energy product-launch feel.

**reference_anchors:** UX research decks, editorial survey graphics, museum-label color systems, refined qualitative analysis visuals.

**balance_guidance:** Lilac and mauve form the main field (~40%). Ash gray should carry neutral structure and baselines (~25-35%). Coral is the warm result or preference accent; berry is reserved for strong negative or contrastive findings.

**common_mistakes:** Using every purple as an accent. Letting gray dominate until the diagram feels disabled. Using coral for routine nodes instead of actual result emphasis.

**canvas:** `#FAF9FB`
**border_default:** `#2D2F3A`
**accent_edge:** `#6F6EA8`
