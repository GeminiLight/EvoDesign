## aurora_cool

**display_name:** Aurora Cool

**digest:** Tier A / 推荐优先 — modern cold-tone AI, network, heatmap-like, embedding, and exploratory system diagrams.

**mood:** Mint, aqua, blue, and violet like a restrained aurora. More energetic than Cool Mist but still polished.

**use_when:** AI systems, embedding and network diagrams, exploratory technical maps, cold-tone dashboards, and figures that need a modern glow without neon excess.

**avoid_when:** Conservative legal/finance diagrams, clinical protocols, or warm human stories where cool futurism feels off.

**reference_anchors:** Modern AI product surfaces, aurora-inspired editorial visuals, technical network explainers, contemporary visualization notebooks.

**balance_guidance:** Mint and aqua should be broad support (~40%). Blue carries computation and structure. Violet is for model/reasoning/policy nodes. Orchid is a rare accent for the surprising or emergent part of the system.

**common_mistakes:** Treating every color as equally loud. Making the palette look cyberpunk by darkening the canvas. Using orchid for routine decoration.

**canvas:** `#F6FBFA`
**border_default:** `#20343D`
**accent_edge:** `#4D82B8`
