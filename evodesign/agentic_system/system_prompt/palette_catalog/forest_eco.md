## forest_eco

**display_name:** Forest Eco Soft

**digest:** Tier B / 特定领域 — ESG, agriculture, sustainability, circular economy; softened earth tones.

**mood:** A forest floor in autumn — sage and moss anchored by burnt-orange leaves on cream paper.

**use_when:** ESG reporting, agricultural and supply-chain diagrams, biodiversity flows, sustainability roadmaps, circular-economy architectures — anywhere `earth-toned, grounded, organic` should read.

**avoid_when:** Pure technical engineering (reads as off-key), AI/SaaS modernity (too earthy), kid-facing education (not playful enough), finance and banking (not authoritative enough). When the topic is digital or financial, the earth tones feel costumed.

**reference_anchors:** Patagonia annual reports, IPCC infographic style, FAO supply-chain diagrams, B Corp impact reports, National Geographic infographics.

**balance_guidance:** Sage and moss carry the `natural` lead voice (~50% combined). Earth (burnt-orange) for highlights and seasonal accents — focal nodes, harvest or outcome moments (~15%). Clay for warm-neutral containers. Sky for water/atmosphere references. The diagram should feel like a forest floor with one bright leaf, not a salad of equal hues.

**common_mistakes:** Oversaturating earth (it's the seasonal accent, not the narrator). Pairing sage with sky in a way that flattens the green-blue distinction. Using this for non-natural content — it will read as forced or `eco-washed`.

**canvas:** `#FAF8EF`
