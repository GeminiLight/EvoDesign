## saas_indigo_neon

**display_name:** SaaS Indigo Soft

**digest:** Tier B / 可用但克制 — modern SaaS and AI products when a cooler product identity is needed.

**mood:** Modern SaaS polish with softened indigo and cyan. Still contemporary, but no longer neon-first.

**use_when:** AI product architectures, modern SaaS dashboards, dev-tool ecosystems, infrastructure-as-code architecture — anywhere the mood should be current, polished, and lightly futuristic without shouting.

**avoid_when:** Traditional finance, healthcare-clinical, education-for-kids, or heavily regulated industries that want institutional neutrality. Prefer `ocean_glass` for calmer system diagrams.

**reference_anchors:** Stripe Press, Linear product pages, Vercel docs, Modal and Replit dashboards, Anthropic developer console.

**balance_guidance:** Indigo is the lead voice (~35-45%). Cyan is now a precise action accent, not a neon wash. Violet for AI/ML and policy nodes. Slate for supporting infrastructure. Emerald for success and output. Leave plenty of neutral space.

**common_mistakes:** Painting the whole diagram indigo + cyan creates default SaaS sameness. Using violet for general nodes. Adding neon green, electric blue, or bright red from outside the palette.

**canvas:** `#F7F8FC`
**border_default:** `#0B1028`
**accent_edge:** `#4077B8`
