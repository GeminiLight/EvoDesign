## coral_warmth

**display_name:** Coral Warmth

**digest:** Customer journeys, marketing funnels, lifestyle brands, B2C onboarding.

**mood:** Sunset on a warm beach — coral and apricot wrapped around a teal anchor. Optimistic, branded, customer-facing.

**use_when:** Customer journeys, marketing funnels, lifestyle-brand process diagrams, B2C onboarding flows — anywhere optimistic warmth should read as the brand voice.

**avoid_when:** Technical engineering content, regulated industries, scientific publications, enterprise system-of-record. The warmth reads as inappropriate when the topic is cold or analytical.

**reference_anchors:** Airbnb host journey, Glossier product pages, Headspace onboarding, Calm app flows, Squarespace marketing pages.

**balance_guidance:** Coral and peach can dominate as the warm narrative voice (~50% combined). Cream provides supporting structure. Teal is the cool anchor — focal nodes, `the moment of decision/conversion` (~15%). Rose for highlights and emotional peaks. The diagram should feel like a beach sunset, not a noon-day glare.

**common_mistakes:** Painting every node coral (peach + cream provide essential modulation). Ignoring teal (it's the anchor that keeps the palette from drifting saccharine). Using this palette for cold or analytical content — the warmth will read as forced.

**canvas:** `#FFF7ED`
**border_default:** `#3D2C25`
**accent_edge:** `#0F766E`
