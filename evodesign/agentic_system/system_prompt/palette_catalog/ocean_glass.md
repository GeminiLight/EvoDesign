## ocean_glass

**display_name:** Ocean Glass

**digest:** Tier A / 推荐优先 — systems, pipelines, agents, trade-offs, data flow, and calm technical narratives.

**mood:** Clear coastal glass: teal, seafoam, blue-gray, and a small warm coral signal. Crisp but not cold.

**use_when:** System diagrams, agent workflows, data pipelines, architecture narratives, efficiency/trade-off figures, operational flows, and technical processes that should feel current and readable.

**avoid_when:** Strongly emotional consumer journeys, formal finance/legal decks, or diagrams where every category must have equal categorical salience.

**reference_anchors:** Contemporary developer docs, coastal editorial palettes, cloud architecture explainers, modern analytics product diagrams.

**balance_guidance:** Teal and seafoam are the resting voice (~45%). Blue-gray supports infrastructure and neutral containers (~25%). Sand and coral should be sparse warm signals for outcomes, exceptions, or user-facing moments. The diagram should feel lucid, not tropical.

**common_mistakes:** Making coral the default, which turns a system palette into a marketing palette. Overusing aqua fills until all nodes merge. Adding bright blues from outside the palette.
