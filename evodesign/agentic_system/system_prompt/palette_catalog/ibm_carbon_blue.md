## ibm_carbon_blue

**display_name:** IBM Carbon Blue

**digest:** Enterprise SaaS / system-of-record / regulated industries; IBM-Carbon polish.

**mood:** Enterprise-grade clarity. Pure white canvas, crisp IBM blue, a single magenta alert hue — system-of-record reliability.

**use_when:** Enterprise SaaS dashboards, system-of-record diagrams, government and financial backend architectures, regulated industries — anywhere IBM-style design language should read as `regulated, audited, dependable`.

**avoid_when:** Consumer products, creative or playful content, anything where the mood should feel intimate or improvised. Carbon's neutrality reads as cold there. Also avoid for purely visual/aesthetic content where its discipline becomes a liability.

**reference_anchors:** IBM Cloud docs, Carbon Design System examples, Maximo and MQ admin consoles, Watson admin UIs.

**balance_guidance:** Blue is the default identity (~40-50% of nodes). Teal carries supporting infrastructure and data layers. Purple highlights decision and policy nodes (it is NOT a generic accent here). Magenta is the alert color and should appear at most ~10% of nodes — Carbon's red-equivalent. Cool-gray for containment and supporting cast.

**common_mistakes:** Promoting magenta beyond critical paths (Carbon's discipline is what makes it read as enterprise). Substituting purple for general accents (it has a specific decision/governance meaning here). Heavy use of cool-gray for primary nodes (it should aggregate, not narrate).

**canvas:** `#FFFFFF`
**border_default:** `#161616`
**accent_edge:** `#9F1853`
