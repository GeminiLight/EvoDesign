## porcelain_editorial

**display_name:** Porcelain Editorial

**digest:** Tier B / 保守精致 — refined categorical diagrams, executive explainers, publication-safe neutral style.

**mood:** Porcelain white with softened blue, mauve, sage, ochre, and lavender. Conservative, but not stale.

**use_when:** Executive explainers, publication-safe diagrams, structured reports, neutral multi-section overviews, and categorical layouts that need refinement more than personality.

**avoid_when:** Highly playful content, brand-heavy marketing, or diagrams that need a strong single accent to dominate the page.

**reference_anchors:** Editorial magazine charts, museum wayfinding palettes, restrained annual reports, classic information-design systems.

**balance_guidance:** Blue and sage are the stable voices. Mauve and lavender add soft distinction. Ochre is a restrained warm accent for decisions or outcomes. Use the palette as a balanced set, not a rainbow.

**common_mistakes:** Overusing ochre until it looks old-fashioned. Treating mauve as a warning color. Mixing in saturated default colors that break the porcelain quality.
