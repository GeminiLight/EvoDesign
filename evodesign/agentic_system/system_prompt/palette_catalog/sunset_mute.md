## sunset_mute

**display_name:** Sunset Mute

**digest:** Tier B / 柔和参考 — before-after stories, gains/losses, milestones, warm emphasis without loud primaries.

**mood:** Muted cream, peach, coral, rose, and berry. Warm narrative contrast with enough depth for emphasis.

**use_when:** Before/after stories, gain/loss narratives, milestones, retrospectives, adoption journeys, and diagrams where warm progression should guide the reader.

**avoid_when:** Pure cool analytical views, accessibility-critical many-category diagrams, or subjects where rose/berry could be misread as risk.

**reference_anchors:** Editorial timeline graphics, soft sunset photography palettes, modern presentation systems, qualitative product-story diagrams.

**balance_guidance:** Cream and peach are broad support (~45%). Coral marks active change. Rose and berry should be used as directional emphasis: rose for notable state, berry for decisive endpoint or tension. Avoid using all five equally.

**common_mistakes:** Making berry the default. Using coral as a generic warning color. Letting the whole diagram become orange; cream and neutral spacing are part of the palette.
