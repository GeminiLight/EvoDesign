## corporate_navy_gold

**display_name:** Corporate Navy Gold

**digest:** Board decks, M&A, banking, legal — boardroom gravitas.

**mood:** Boardroom gravitas. Deep navy authority warmed by sparing classic gold — trust accumulated over decades.

**use_when:** Board decks, M&A architectures, regulatory roadmaps, banking and legal reference diagrams, executive briefings — anywhere `trust accumulated over decades` should read as the dominant voice.

**avoid_when:** AI/SaaS modernity (reads as old-fashioned), healthcare patient UI (too austere), education-for-kids (too formal), creative or marketing content (too rigid). When the audience expects warmth or innovation cues, pick a different palette.

**reference_anchors:** Goldman Sachs reports, McKinsey strategy decks, JPMorgan annual reports, Big-Four audit summaries, Federal Reserve research notes.

**balance_guidance:** Navy is the dominant identity (~50%). Gold reserved for highlights — focal nodes, KPI hubs, `what matters` — at most ~20%. Neutrals carry supporting cast. Sky for information and external feeds. Rose only for compliance/risk callouts (<=5%).

**common_mistakes:** Overusing gold makes the diagram look gaudy (gold is for emphasis, not narration). Mixing gold with sky on the same node (they fight each other). Painting compliance/risk in gold — rose is the disciplined choice for risk; gold is for `the value at stake`.

**canvas:** `#FFFFFF`
**border_default:** `#1A1A1A`
**accent_edge:** `#003366`

### Fill Families

| name | fill | stroke | voice |
|------|------|--------|-------|
| navy | `#DBEAFE` | `#003366` | The voice of authority — primary entities, main-flow anchors, core institutions, the spine of the diagram. |
| gold | `#FEF3C7` | `#92400E` | Reserved highlights — focal nodes, KPIs, `what matters most` (<=20%). Gold should feel earned. |
| neutral | `#F3F4F6` | `#1F2937` | Supporting cast, containers, `everything else`. Quiet enough to let navy and gold do the talking. |
| sky | `#E0F2FE` | `#0369A1` | Information, external counterparties, market data, third-party feeds. |
| rose | `#FFE4E6` | `#9F1239` | Compliance flags, risk callouts. Use sparingly — rose should mean `someone in legal will read this`. |
