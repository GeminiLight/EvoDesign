## blossom_warm

**display_name:** Blossom Warm

**digest:** Tier B / 柔和参考 — warm human-centered flows, preference, onboarding, qualitative panels, positive affect.

**mood:** Peach, coral, muted yellow, and warm tan. Friendly and human without default orange harshness.

**use_when:** Human-centered flows, onboarding, preference summaries, feedback loops, qualitative panels, service journeys, and diagrams where warmth should reduce friction.

**avoid_when:** Dense technical architecture, high-stakes compliance, clinical protocols, or diagrams where warm color could imply marketing rather than analysis.

**reference_anchors:** Modern service-design boards, soft editorial infographics, hospitality UX maps, restrained lifestyle product explainers.

**balance_guidance:** Peach and petal carry the main warmth (~40%). Apricot and wheat provide variation for steps and decisions. Taupe is the stabilizer; use it for neutral structure so the palette does not become candy-like.

**common_mistakes:** Saturating coral until it looks like an error state. Using wheat/yellow for every decision. Forgetting taupe, which is what makes the palette mature.
