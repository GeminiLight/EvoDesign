## playful_primary

**display_name:** Playful Soft

**digest:** Tier C / 特定场景 — friendly education and onboarding; softened from primary-color defaults.

**mood:** Friendly classroom energy with softened sky, leaf, butter, peach, and rose. Playful without raw primary-color glare.

**use_when:** K-12 explainers, children's-content infographics, friendly onboarding, beginner-tutorial diagrams — anywhere a cheerful, welcoming energy should greet the reader.

**avoid_when:** Enterprise and finance, healthcare-clinical, AI/SaaS engineering, regulatory documentation, or any high-stakes topic. When credibility matters more than welcome, choose `blush_sage`, `ocean_glass`, or `porcelain_editorial`.

**reference_anchors:** Khan Academy diagrams, Crash Course graphics, BrainPOP characters, Sesame Street curriculum sketches, Code.org illustrations.

**balance_guidance:** Use multiple families freely, but keep butter and rose from dominating. Sky and leaf should carry the clearest educational structure. Peach can mark examples. Rose is for memorable moments only.

**common_mistakes:** Reintroducing saturated primary red/yellow/blue. Using rose as a general category. Pairing playful colors with overly austere shape choices.

**canvas:** `#FFF9E8`
**border_default:** `#1F2937`
**accent_edge:** `#D99A3A`
